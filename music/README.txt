Royalty-free music for reels
============================

Drop audio files here (.mp3, .m4a, .wav, .aac, .ogg, .flac). The reel pipeline
rotates through whatever is in this folder and avoids recently-used tracks, so
add as many as you like — more tracks = less repetition.

RECOMMENDED SOURCE: Pixabay Music  (https://pixabay.com/music/)
  - Free, no attribution required, safe for a monetized page.
  - Steps:
      1. Sign in (free) at pixabay.com.
      2. Open https://pixabay.com/music/ and pick a mood (e.g. "Calm",
         "Beautiful Plays", "Ambient", "Acoustic", "Cinematic").
      3. Click Download on tracks you like (no attribution needed).
      4. Move the downloaded .mp3 files into THIS folder.
  - Grab ~50 for good variety. You can add or remove tracks anytime.

Other safe options: YouTube Audio Library (filter to "No attribution required"),
or your own cleared/owned audio.

Avoid: chart/commercial songs and "trending" app audio — those can get the reel
muted or hurt monetization. Trending in-app sounds can only be added manually in
the Facebook/Instagram app, not via automated posting.
